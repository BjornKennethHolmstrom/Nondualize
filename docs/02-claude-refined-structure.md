**Absolutely do not dismiss the intellectual gateway!** Here's why it's essential:

### The Intellect as Preparation
The Yellow mind's ability to see systems, paradoxes, and interconnections is **magnificent preparation** for non-dual recognition. When you intellectually understand that:
- All dualities co-arise
- The observer and observed are inseparable
- The self is a construct with no fixed boundary

...you've cleared enormous ground. The intellect can dismantle the grosser illusions even if it can't provide the final recognition.

### The Integration Function

### The Trust-Building Function
Many seekers (especially Yellow thinkers) won't trust purely experiential/mystical approaches. They need to see the **logic** of non-duality first. Once they trust the framework intellectually, they're more willing to try the direct methods.
